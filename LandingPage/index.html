Questions

  
Is it safe to install? Why “Open Anyway”?
OpenNotch is open source — every line is on GitHub — and builds are signed with the same certificate every release. They aren't notarised by Apple yet (that needs a paid developer account), so macOS asks you to confirm once in System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway. Anything risky the app does still waits for your OK.

  
Is it really free?
Yes. OpenNotch is MIT-licensed and open source. You pay only for the AI you choose — or nothing, with a local model.

  
Does it work on a Mac without a notch?
Yes — it shows a virtual notch. It needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later; Liquid Glass and the Apple on-device model need macOS 26 (on macOS 27, newer Macs get Apple's bigger on-device model automatically).

  
Can it send emails or messages on its own?
No. It drafts replies in Mail, and it sends an email only when you ask — and you approve that exact email every time. It never sends messages. Commands, file changes, calendar edits and connector tools wait for your approval too.

  
How is it different from Siri or a chatbot?
Siri mostly answers and waits to be asked; a chatbot lives in a window. OpenNotch works across your Mac's apps — mail, calendar, files, documents — reaches out when something needs you, and shows every step it took. It's free, open source and works with any AI.

  
Can I see what it actually did?
Yes — ⋯ › Developer shows every task as a timeline: the model, each tool with what it sent and got back, approvals and timings. It stays on your Mac for 14 days.

  
Where is my data?
In ~/Library/Application Support/OpenNotch on your Mac, with keys in the Keychain. There's no account and no telemetry.

  
How do I add more tools?
Add any MCP server to mcp.json (Settings › AI › Edit mcp.json). Its tools appear to the assistant automatically.
