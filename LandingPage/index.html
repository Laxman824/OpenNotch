Local-first

Your data stays on your Mac.

  • Chats, memory, clipboard and settings are saved locally
  • API keys are stored in your macOS Keychain
  • No account, no telemetry, no OpenNotch servers
  • The only traffic goes to the AI you pick — or go fully offline with Ollama, LM Studio or Apple on-device

Weather uses Open-Meteo, and web fetches happen only when you ask for them.

Questions

  
Is it really free?
Yes. OpenNotch is MIT-licensed and open source. You pay only for the AI you choose — or nothing, with a local model.

  
Does it work on a Mac without a notch?
Yes — it shows a virtual notch. It needs macOS 14 Sonoma or later; Liquid Glass and the Apple on-device model need macOS 26.

  
Can it send emails or messages on its own?
No. It drafts replies in Mail; nothing is ever sent. Commands, file changes, calendar edits and connector tools wait for your approval.

  
Where is my data?
In ~/Library/Application Support/OpenNotch on your Mac, with keys in the Keychain. There's no account and no telemetry.

  
How do I add more tools?
Add any MCP server to mcp.json (Settings › AI › Edit mcp.json). Its tools appear to the assistant automatically.
